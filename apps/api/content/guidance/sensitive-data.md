*A plain-language restatement of the University's Information Classification
Standard and the Provost's Staff Generative AI Usage Guidance. Those
documents are the authority — when in doubt, ask before you paste.*

## The four tiers

Every University tool page describes what it accepts using the same
classification. Learn it once and the rest of the rules follow.

| Tier | What it covers | In an approved AI tool? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tier 0 — Public** | Published budgets, posted policies, public web content | Yes |
| **Tier 1 — Business** | Internal budgets, memos, contact lists, draft SOPs | Yes |
| **Tier 2 — Confidential** | Personnel files, FERPA records, NDA and vendor-confidential content | Yes in Copilot; check external obligations first |
| **Tier 3 — Restricted** | SSNs, bank and payment card data, PHI, passwords | **Never** |

The phrasing to use everywhere: **Tier 1 and 2; never Tier 3.**

## Approved tool ≠ any tool

[Copilot Chat](/guides/copilot-chat) and licensed
[Microsoft 365 Copilot](/guides/microsoft-365-copilot) keep your data inside
UNC's Microsoft tenant, and Microsoft does not use it to train models. A
free public chatbot does neither. **The same document that is fine in
Copilot can be a policy violation in a consumer tool.** Check the tool
registry before you paste; if a tool is not listed there, do not use it for
work data.

## Two rules that override the tier

**External obligations win.** Grant terms, contracts, data use agreements,
and system security plans can restrict data that the tier table would
otherwise allow. Tier 2 does not mean "cleared" if a contract says
otherwise.

**Prompts and outputs are public records.** Under G.S. 132-1, what you type
into a University-provisioned AI tool and what it produces — including Zoom
AI Companion meeting summaries — is a public record, subject to retention
and to release on request. Use University accounts, and write as though the
prompt could be read back.

## Never, in any AI tool

- Anything Tier 3: SSNs, bank or payment card details, passwords, keys
- Protected health information or anything covered by HIPAA
- **Hiring, evaluation, or discipline decisions about employees.** The Staff
  Generative AI Usage Guidance prohibits this outright. AI may help you
  *structure* feedback you have already written; it must not produce
  ratings, screen candidates, or inform a disciplinary outcome.

## Getting a new tool approved

If your unit wants an AI tool that is not in the registry, or a vendor turns
on an AI feature in a system you already use, it goes through the University
Data Assistance form → Information Security Office review → the Data
Governance Oversight Group. Start with your unit's Senior IT contact. Do not
buy first.

## The 10-second test

Before pasting, ask: *"Which tier is this, and is it Tier 3?"* If you cannot
answer the first half, that is your signal to check rather than paste.
