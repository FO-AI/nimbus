## Start with these two

ITS recommends two **LinkedIn Learning** courses, free for UNC employees and
the fastest route from nothing to competent:

- **Learning Microsoft 365 Copilot** — what it does in each app, and where it
  does not help
- **Prompt Engineering** — the general skill, which transfers to whichever
  tool your unit ends up using

Activate LinkedIn Learning with your Onyen at
[software.sites.unc.edu/linkedin](https://software.sites.unc.edu/linkedin/).
Completions flow to your **Carolina Talent** transcript, so they count toward
a professional development goal rather than disappearing.

## Then pick by how you learn

**Reading and doing, on your own** — Microsoft Learn's
[Get started with Microsoft 365 Copilot](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/paths/get-started-with-microsoft-365-copilot)
is three modules at business-user level, free and no sign-up. It pairs
directly with the [playbooks in this library](/guides).

**With a person** — the
[University Library AI Studio](https://library.unc.edu/ai/library-ai-studio/)
runs workshops and one-to-one consultations, including hands-on
[PromptLab](/guides/promptlab) sessions. Email libraryai@unc.edu. This is the
right option if you have a specific piece of work you want help restructuring.

**By trying it on real work** — the fastest path for most F&O staff. Take a
[prompt from the library](/prompts), run it on something you actually have to
produce this week, and adapt it. [How to write a prompt](/guides/how-to-write-a-prompt)
covers the CLEAR framework for when the first result is not what you wanted.

## Recording what you finish

[Carolina Talent](https://go.unc.edu/carolinatalent/) is where OHR records
learning. LinkedIn Learning completions arrive there automatically once your
account is activated with your Onyen; other training you add yourself. If AI
capability is in your performance goals, this is the evidence.

## If your unit wants to go further

The [AI Acceleration Program](https://aiacceleration.unc.edu/) is the route
for units that want to build something — a Copilot Studio agent, Azure
resources — rather than use what exists. Grants are open to staff. Read
[getting an AI tool approved](/guides/getting-a-tool-approved) first, because
the approval path applies either way.

## Before any of it

Training teaches you what the tools can do, not what you are allowed to put
in them. [What data can I put into an AI tool](/guides/sensitive-data) is the
five minutes that keeps a course from turning into an incident.
