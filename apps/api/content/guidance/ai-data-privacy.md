## The distinction that matters

Two things look identical on screen and are not the same:

**Signed in with your Onyen** — Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat with Data
Protection. Your prompts and the responses are covered by the University's
agreement with Microsoft: not retained to train the public model, not visible
to other tenants.

**Signed out, or signed in with a personal account** — the consumer product.
The agreement does not apply. What you paste may be retained and may be used
to improve the vendor's models.

The protection comes from *your Onyen sign-in*, not from the page you are on.
Check which account you are in before you paste anything that is not already
public.

## What this rules out

Any AI tool the University has not assessed is off-limits for University data
regardless of how good it is — the free note-taker that joins your meetings,
the browser extension that summarizes your inbox, the vendor AI feature
somebody switched on last week. If it has not been through
[the approval path](/guides/getting-a-tool-approved), it does not get
University data.

## What protection does not mean

"Covered by the agreement" is not the same as "safe for anything":

- **Tier 3 is still out.** SSNs, bank and card data, PHI, credentials — the
  contract does not change the classification.
- **Copilot sees what you already see.** It can surface a file you had access
  to but had forgotten about, including in a summary you then forward.
- **Prompts and outputs are public records.** See
  [AI and the Public Records Act](/guides/ai-and-public-records).

## If you think something went into the wrong tool

Tell your Senior IT contact or the ITS Help Portal. Reporting it early is
routine; finding out later is not.
