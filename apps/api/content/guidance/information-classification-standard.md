## Why this page exists

Every tool entry in Nimbus states a data tier, and every prompt warns you off
Tier 3. Those tiers are not a Nimbus invention — they come from the University
Information Classification Standard, which classifies **all** University data,
not just what goes into AI tools.

This page points you at the standard. For the working version aimed at what
you can paste into Copilot, read
[what data can I put into an AI tool](/guides/sensitive-data) instead.

## The tiers, in F&O terms

| Tier | Name | Typical F&O examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0** | Public | Published budget books, the annual financial report, posted policies |
| **1** | Business | Internal budgets, memos, contact lists, routine correspondence |
| **2** | Confidential | Personnel files, FERPA records, NDA and vendor-confidential material |
| **3** | Restricted | SSNs, bank and payment card data, PHI, passwords and keys |

## Two things people get wrong

**The tier belongs to the data, not the document.** One Tier 3 field makes the
whole spreadsheet Tier 3. A budget workbook with a column of bank details is
not a Tier 1 budget workbook.

**A contract can raise the tier.** Vendor-confidential terms and grant
conditions can bind data that would otherwise be Tier 1. Where an external
obligation and the tier disagree, the obligation wins.

## Who decides

Data stewards classify data for their area, and your unit's Senior IT contact
is the practical route to an answer. If you cannot work out the tier, that is
the signal to ask rather than paste.
