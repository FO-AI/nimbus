## Start here

If you read one page before using AI for University work, read this one. The
Provost's guidance is what the rest of Nimbus is built on — the tools in the
registry, the tier rules, and the screening applied to every prompt in the
library all come back to it.

There is **no numbered University policy on generative AI**. This guidance is
the governance, and Nimbus says so rather than implying a policy number that
does not exist.

## What it permits

Work where AI drafts and you decide:

- Drafting budgets, reports, memos, and letters
- Data analysis on non-sensitive data
- Scheduling and workload planning
- Transcription and meeting minutes
- Flagging possible compliance issues **for a human to review**

## What it prohibits outright

**AI may not be used to hire, evaluate, or discipline employees.** Not to
screen candidates, not to produce a rating, not to build the record behind a
disciplinary action. This is the hard line, and it is why a good deal of the
external prompt libraries was screened out before import — see
[Using AI in performance management](/guides/ai-in-performance-management)
for what a supervisor may still do.

## The conditions attached

- **Sensitive data needs approval first.** Not a judgment call: an
  Information Security Office risk assessment and Data Governance Oversight
  Group approval. See [what data can I put into an AI tool](/guides/sensitive-data).
- **Consult your Senior IT contact before adopting or buying any GenAI
  product.** Including an AI feature switched on inside a system you already
  own — see [getting an AI tool approved](/guides/getting-a-tool-approved).
- **Disclose AI use in decisions with ethical or legal impact.** If AI shaped
  something that affects a person's money, standing, or rights, say so.

## What it means day to day in F&O

You own the output. The guidance treats AI as a drafting assistant, not a
decision-maker — so the reconciliation still gets checked, the memo still gets
read before it goes out, and the numbers Copilot restates still get verified
against the system of record.
