## The point

North Carolina's Public Records Act is broad: records made or received in the
transaction of public business belong to the public, whatever the format. The
School of Government's reading is that AI does not create an exception.

That means **what you type into Copilot about University work is a public
record**, and so is what it types back.

## What this covers in practice

- Prompts and responses in Copilot Chat or PromptLab about University business
- AI-generated meeting summaries and transcripts —
  [Zoom AI Companion](/guides/zoom-ai-companion) recaps, Teams recaps
- Drafts an AI produced that were sent to someone or used in a decision

## Three consequences worth acting on

**Use University accounts.** A public record created in a personal account is
still a public record, and producing it means going into your personal
account. Sign in with your Onyen.

**Retain by content, not by tool.** An AI-generated summary of a budget
meeting is retained on the schedule for budget meeting records. The fact that
software wrote it changes nothing about how long it is kept.

**Write as though it will be read.** A meeting recap you never edited can be
produced in a records request with its errors intact. Recaps that matter get
reviewed and corrected before they are filed or forwarded.

## Tell people they are being recorded

An AI notetaker is a recording, and participants should know before it starts.
Zoom AI Companion is off by default and the person enabling it is the one
responsible for saying so.

## When a request arrives

Route it the way you would any public records request — through University
Counsel and your unit's records contact. Do not delete AI-generated material
because it is rough, and do not decide on your own that a prompt log is not a
record.
