## What it is

Copilot Chat is an institutionally-scoped chat assistant — the same
underlying models as the consumer product, but running under UNC's agreement
with Microsoft. It was previously called Bing Chat Enterprise.

The distinction that matters: when you are signed in with your UNC account,
Microsoft does not store or view your chats and does not use UNC data to
train its models. The consumer version of Copilot, ChatGPT, and Gemini all
do retain what you type. Same-looking tool, entirely different data posture.

## Getting to it

Sign in at [m365.cloud.microsoft/chat](https://m365.cloud.microsoft/chat/)
using **onyen@ad.unc.edu** as your email address — the same address you use
for Heelmail. If you are not signed in with that account, you are using the
consumer version and the protections above do not apply.

Everyone with an active Onyen has the **basic** license. Basic cannot see
your Microsoft 365 files; it works from what you paste into the chat. Some
staff hold a **premium** license, which does reach your files and appears
inside Word, Excel, and the other apps — that is covered by the
[licensed Copilot entry](/guides/microsoft-365-copilot).

## What data you can put in

Tier 1 and Tier 2 data are covered. **Never Tier 3, and never PHI or
anything subject to HIPAA.** Grant terms, contracts, data use agreements,
and system security plans override the "safe for Tier 1 and 2" designation —
if your data carries an external obligation, check it before you paste. See
[what data can I put into an AI tool](/guides/sensitive-data).
