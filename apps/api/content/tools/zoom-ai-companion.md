## What it is

AI Companion generates a meeting summary and a list of next steps at the end
of a Zoom call. It is **off by default** — the host turns it on, per
meeting.

For Finance & Operations that is a real time saver on budget reviews, vendor
calls, and project standups, where the notes are the deliverable and nobody
wants to be the person typing instead of participating.

## Before you switch it on

**Tell the participants.** Summaries and transcripts are generated from what
everyone in the meeting says, and people should know before they speak.

**Assume it is a public record.** Under G.S. 132-1, an AI-generated meeting
summary created on a University account is a public record like any other
document — subject to retention rules and to release on request. That
changes what belongs in the meeting, not just what belongs in the notes. Do
not enable it for conversations about personnel matters, contract
negotiations still under seal, or anything you would not want read back
verbatim.

## Practical use

- Enable it for recurring operational meetings where the summary is the
  point
- Skip it for one-to-one conversations and anything involving named
  individuals
- Always edit the summary before circulating — it captures what was said,
  not what was decided, and the difference matters in a close package or a
  vendor file
- The
  [meeting notes to action items prompt](/prompts/meeting-notes-to-actions)
  turns a raw summary into something you can actually send
