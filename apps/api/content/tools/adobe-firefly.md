## What it is

Firefly generates images and text effects, and is built into the Adobe apps.
Adobe trained it on stock images, openly licensed material, and public-
domain content, which is why it carries less copyright risk than image
generators trained on scraped web content.

## Why it is in this registry

Mostly for completeness — it is one of the tools the University lists as
available, so staff find it and ask. Genuine Finance & Operations uses are
narrow: illustrations for a unit newsletter, a graphic for an internal
training deck, placeholder art in a presentation.

If you are producing anything public-facing, route it through your unit's
communications staff. AI-generated imagery in official University materials
is a brand and accessibility question, not just a design one — generated
images still need alt text written by a human.

## Access

Through the
[UNC Creative Cloud subscription](https://software.sites.unc.edu/adobe/).
Free for students and instructional faculty and staff; $25 per year for
non-instructional faculty and staff.
