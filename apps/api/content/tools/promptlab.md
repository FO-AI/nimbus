## What it is

PromptLab is the Library's front end to several commercial models at once —
OpenAI's GPT, Google's Gemini, and Anthropic's Claude — behind a single UNC
login. Free with an Onyen.

Its value for Finance & Operations is comparison. When an output from one
model reads oddly, running the same prompt through another model is the
quickest way to tell whether the problem is the prompt or the model. It is
also the only approved way to reach non-Microsoft models with University
work.

For everyday drafting and summarizing, [Copilot Chat](/guides/copilot-chat)
is the better default — it is the tool most F&O workflows and playbooks
assume.

## What data you can put in

**Tier 1 and 2; never Tier 3, and never PHI or anything covered by HIPAA.**
Same ceiling as Copilot — see
[what data can I put into an AI tool](/guides/sensitive-data). External
obligations in grants, contracts, and data use agreements override the tier
either way.

Two University pages describe this differently: the Library's platform
comparison guide lists **Tier 2**, while the Provost's
[AI Tools at UNC](https://ai.unc.edu/tools/) page lists Tier 0 and Tier 1.
Nimbus follows the Library, which runs PromptLab and classifies its own
service. If you see the other figure quoted somewhere on campus, that is why.

For a specific workflow you are unsure about — particularly anything under an
award or a vendor agreement — ask libraryai@unc.edu before you rely on it.

## Getting help

The [Library AI Studio](https://library.unc.edu/ai/library-ai-studio/) runs
workshops and one-on-one consultations, and its
[privacy documentation](https://library.unc.edu/documentation/promptlab-privacy-information/)
explains how PromptLab handles what you type.
