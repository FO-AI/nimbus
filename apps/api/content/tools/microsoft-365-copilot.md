## What it is

The licensed tier of Copilot — ITS calls it Copilot Chat **premium**, and
Microsoft has also called it Copilot for Microsoft 365. Unlike the free
basic tier, it has access to your Microsoft 365 files and appears inside the
apps themselves: a Copilot pane in Word, Excel, Outlook, Teams, and
PowerPoint.

That file access is the whole point for Finance & Operations work. It is
what lets Copilot analyze a workbook you already have open, summarize a mail
thread in place, or build a deck from a report — instead of you pasting
content into a chat window.

## Who has it

Not everyone. Licenses are paid (roughly $20 per user per month) and are
requested by departments through their **local IT staff**, not centrally. To
check what you have, sign in to Copilot Chat with onyen@ad.unc.edu — your
license type is shown next to your profile icon.

If you only have basic, you can still do most drafting and summarizing work
by pasting content into [Copilot Chat](/guides/copilot-chat).

## What data you can put in

Same rule as Copilot Chat: **Tier 1 and Tier 2 data; never Tier 3, PHI, or
HIPAA-covered data.** Having a license does not widen what is allowed — it
widens what Copilot can reach, which makes the boundary more important, not
less. External obligations in grants, contracts, and data use agreements
override the tier designation. See
[what data can I put into an AI tool](/guides/sensitive-data).
